Results (plots/LunarLander-v3_training-curves)
For both DQN and PPO the RND version performs better. The improvement is greater for DQN.
PPO starts off with a better exploration method than DQN.
PPO does exploration through the entropy bonus and the stochastic policy, while DQN only sometimes
takes a random action instead of following the policy. This leaves less room for improvement for PPO.
