Results: plots/Snapshot_STEPS_v2.png
The plots show heatmaps of the intrinsic reward for the x and y values of the states visited in 
the first STEPS steps of the training (STEPS = 1000, 10000, 50000, 150000, 300000).

The heatmaps show that the regions with the highest intrinsic rewards remain mostly the same 
throughout training. As training progresses, the absolute value of the intrinsic rewards 
decreases and the rewards become less concentrated in a single spot, spreading more evenly 
across the surrounding area.

This shows that the novelty of frequently visited states is reduced while exploration 
expands to nearby states.
