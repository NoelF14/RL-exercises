Observations (from dqn_training_curves_l1.png):
Buffer size: Large buffer started out better, but small buffer overtook the large buffer in the end
Network depth: Shallow network performed better than deep network (big difference at 10000 to 15000 frames)
Network width: Wide network started out better, but narrow network overtook the wide network in the end
Batch size: Overall large batch performed better than small batch
--> In this environment, simpler configurations often outperform more complex ones. 
    This may be because CartPole is a relatively simple task, where high model capacity is not necessary. 
    As a result, more complex models do not provide additional representational benefit 
    and can even make optimization harder, leading to slower or less stable learning.
