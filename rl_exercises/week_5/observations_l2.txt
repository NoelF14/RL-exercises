-  How does the length of the trajectories affect the training?
    (Episodes per trajectory: 1,5,10; total episodes: 1000,5000)
    1 episode: Ep 1000 Steps 26983, AvgReturn  72.2 ± 23.1
    1 episode: Ep 5000 Steps 827957, AvgReturn 500.0 ±  0.0
    5 episodes: Ep 1000 Steps 21060, AvgReturn   8.6 ±  0.8
    5 episodes: Ep 5000 Steps 123452, AvgReturn 129.6 ± 44.6
    10 episodes: Ep 5000 Steps 109956, AvgReturn  34.4 ±  4.1
    --> longer trajectories lead to slower training (in terms of episodes needed)
        because there are less updates.
